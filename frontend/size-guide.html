THE RIGHT FIT

Start with the fit.

Use this simple measuring routine, then compare your result with the size labels shown on the individual BF SHOES product.

Measure at home

  1. Place a sheet of paper on a hard, level floor against a wall.
  2. Stand on the paper with your heel lightly touching the wall.
  3. Mark the tip of your longest toe and measure from the wall edge to the mark.
  4. Repeat for both feet and use the longer measurement when comparing fit.
  5. Measure later in the day if your feet tend to swell during normal activity.

Before you choose

Use the size system and size options displayed on the product listing. Shoe shapes and fit can differ between styles; a size label is not a guarantee that every design will fit identically.

BF SHOES does not publish a universal centimetre-to-UK conversion chart here because product lasts and sizing can vary. Check the specific product’s available sizes before ordering.

Fit check

  • Allow comfortable space in front of the longest toe.
  • Your heel should feel secure without painful rubbing.
  • The widest part of your foot should not feel pinched.
  • Try both shoes on a clean, level surface and walk a few steps.

If a product page does not provide enough sizing detail, wait for the store to confirm it rather than guessing.
