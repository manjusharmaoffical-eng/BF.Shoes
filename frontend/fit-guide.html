COMFORT FIRST

A better fit, step by step.

A good fit should feel secure and comfortable. Use these checks alongside the size and product information shown on each listing.

Check the shape

Compare the product description and photos with how you plan to wear the shoes. A narrow-looking toe, open sandal or structured upper may feel different from a relaxed everyday style.

Try them safely

  • Try shoes with the socks you expect to wear.
  • Check both feet; one may be slightly larger.
  • Walk indoors on a clean surface and check for pressure points.
  • Do not keep wearing a pair that causes pain, numbness or rubbing.

Need more detail?

Product-specific fit notes should come from the product listing or be confirmed by the BF SHOES team. Do not rely on a universal fit rule for every style.
