THE DETAILS

Store policies.

India delivery currently covers PIN code 500052 with a flat ₹55 delivery charge. Return and exchange requests must be raised within 12 hours after delivery, subject to applicable consumer law.

Shipping & delivery

BF SHOES currently supports India delivery to PIN code 500052. A flat delivery charge of ₹55 (INR) applies to each eligible India order. India checkout remains disabled while INR product prices, GST details and PayU merchant credentials and callback verification are completed. PayU Hosted Checkout is the selected India payment provider. Delivery to other PIN codes is not currently supported.

Orders are subject to stock and delivery validation before confirmation. For delivery questions, contact the store using the verified contact details supplied by BF SHOES.

Returns & refunds

Submit a return or exchange request within 12 hours after the order is marked delivered. Items should be unused and in their original packaging unless the issue is a defect or an incorrect item. Contact BF SHOES before sending an item back so the team can confirm eligibility and the return address. Any return shipping or pickup cost will be disclosed before a return is arranged. This policy does not limit consumer rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded, including applicable rights for defective or incorrect products.

Privacy

Checkout information such as name, email, phone number and delivery address is used to process orders, arrange delivery and handle customer support. Do not submit information that is not needed for your order. Data retention periods, processors, hosting locations, data-subject request contact and any analytics/email providers must be confirmed and added before this notice is considered complete.

Terms of sale

Product availability and prices are confirmed by the live catalog. An order is subject to stock validation and store confirmation. Payment methods will be shown only after the relevant merchant account and payment flow are verified. Checkout is currently unavailable. The store owner must add the registered business identity, governing terms, complaint contact and other required local disclosures before publication.

Contact

Use the store’s verified customer-support contact. A placeholder email address is intentionally not shown as a working contact.
