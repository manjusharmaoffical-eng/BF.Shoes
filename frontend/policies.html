Shipping & delivery

BF SHOES currently accepts delivery orders in India only for PIN code 500052, subject to checkout availability and order confirmation. Cash on Delivery is the only enabled payment method.

A flat delivery fee of ₹50 applies to an order. The fee is included in the order total shown at checkout. No delivery fee is charged for an empty cart. Delivery to any other PIN code or country is not currently supported.

Orders are subject to stock and delivery validation before confirmation. For delivery questions, contact the store using the verified contact details supplied by BF SHOES.

Returns & refunds

Contact BF SHOES before returning an item so the team can confirm eligibility and the return address. Items should be unused and in their original packaging unless the issue is a defect or an incorrect item. A ₹100 return fee may apply to eligible returns, subject to confirmation before the return is arranged. The final return window, exceptions, pickup costs and refund process must be checked against applicable consumer law.

Privacy

Checkout information such as name, email, phone number and delivery address is used to process orders, arrange delivery and handle customer support. Do not submit information that is not needed for your order. Data retention periods, processors, hosting locations, data-subject request contact and any analytics/email providers must be confirmed and added before this notice is considered complete.

Terms of sale

Product availability and prices are confirmed by the live catalog. An order is subject to stock validation and store confirmation. Cash on Delivery means payment is collected at delivery; it does not remove the need to verify the order and delivery details. The store owner must add the registered business identity, governing terms, complaint contact and other required local disclosures before publication.
