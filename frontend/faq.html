Good questions. Clear answers.

The essentials about browsing BF SHOES, checking product availability and placing an order.

Which markets does BF SHOES support?

The storefront has separate India (INR) and UAE (AED) catalogues. Select your market to view its catalogue.

Why can a product price or size be unavailable?

Prices and physical stock are shown as available only when the relevant market price and size-level stock have been verified.

Can I place an order now?

Checkout opens only after the relevant market’s price, delivery, tax and payment settings pass verification. Cash on Delivery has the same market and stock safeguards.

Where does BF SHOES currently deliver in India?

The current configured target is PIN code 500052. Other PIN codes are not enabled in the current configuration.

How do I track an order?

Use your order number and the checkout email on the order tracking page.

How do returns work?

Submit a request through your customer account. The BF SHOES team reviews it and confirms the applicable next steps.
