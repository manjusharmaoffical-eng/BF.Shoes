CARE FOR YOUR PAIR

Keep every step looking sharp.

A few careful habits can help maintain your footwear. Always follow any care label or material-specific instructions supplied with your product.

Everyday care

  • Brush away loose dust with a soft, dry cloth or brush.
  • Clean small marks gently and test any cleaner on a discreet area first.
  • Allow damp footwear to air-dry naturally, away from direct heat.
  • Store pairs in a cool, dry place and avoid prolonged compression.
  • Use shoe trees or light paper support only where suitable for the shoe shape.

Different materials need different care

Leather, suede, mesh, knit, synthetic and coated finishes can react differently to water and cleaning products. Do not soak footwear or use solvents, bleach or strong detergents unless the product’s care instructions specifically permit them.

This is general guidance, not a substitute for the care instructions supplied with a particular pair. Check the product details if you are unsure about its material.
