AFTER YOUR ORDER

We’ll review it with care.

Return and exchange requests are reviewed by the BF SHOES team. Submitting a request does not automatically issue a refund or change stock.

How to submit a request

  1. Sign in to your BF SHOES customer account.
  2. Open your order history and choose the relevant order.
  3. Submit a return or exchange request with the item and reason.
  4. Wait for the team to review eligibility and confirm the next steps.
Open customer account

What is not yet confirmed

The return window, eligibility rules, return address, collection or shipping costs and refund timing still require owner confirmation. These terms are not invented here. The team must confirm the applicable process with you in line with local consumer law.

Important

A request is not an approved return. No refund, fee or inventory adjustment is made automatically when a request is submitted.
